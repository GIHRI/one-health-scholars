2026 One Health Scholars

Partner Health Departments

The 2026 cohort places graduate scholars directly within local health departments across Illinois — building a pipeline between university research and public health practice. Each placement is structured around a real departmental need, giving scholars applied field experience while contributing to the LHD's capacity for One Health work.

2026 Partner Departments
LC
Lincoln County Health Department
Lincoln County · Central Illinois
Contact: Dr. Sarah Walsh
Health Administrator

The scholar placement with Lincoln County focuses on building a county-level One Health surveillance framework, contributing to disease vector mapping and community risk communication across Central Illinois rural communities.

CC
Cook County Department of Public Health
Cook County · Northeast Illinois
Contact: Marcus Chen
Community Partnerships Lead

The Cook County placement embeds the scholar within CCDPH's environmental health division, supporting zoonotic disease monitoring and community health assessments across the greater Chicago metropolitan area.

CU
Champaign-Urbana Public Health District
Champaign County · East-Central Illinois
Contact: Dr. Priya Nair
Director of Environmental Health

The CUPHD placement focuses on environmental health assessment in partnership with UIUC research teams, with the scholar contributing to pesticide exposure studies and community health outreach in Champaign County.
