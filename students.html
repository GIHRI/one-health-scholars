2026 One Health Scholars

Student Ambassadors

The 2026 cohort brings together graduate students from across UIUC, UIC, and UIS — selected through a competitive application process for their academic preparation, prior community or research experience, and demonstrated interest in cross-disciplinary public health work. Each scholar is paired with a faculty mentor whose active project aligns with their background and career goals.
