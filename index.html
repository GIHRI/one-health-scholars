The Program

Preparing the next generation of leaders to work where human, animal, and environmental health meet.

The Illinois One Health Scholars Program bridges academic research and public health practice, connecting graduate scholars, university researchers, and Local Health Departments in a shared effort to strengthen health systems and improve community well-being.

Why One Health

Three kinds of health, deeply connected.

The health of people, animals, and the environment does not occur in isolation. Across all three, the 2026 cohort is putting that connection to work.

Human Health
Population health, equity, and community well-being.
Animal Health
Surveillance, zoonotic disease, and shared ecosystems.
Environmental Health
Climate, water, food systems, and the natural world.
The Scholar Journey

One path, four stages.

Every scholar moves from selection, through guided research, into applied public health practice, and ultimately into leadership.

1
Select

A small cohort is chosen through a competitive process for academic achievement, leadership, and a drive to improve community health.

2
Discover

Scholars spend their first months on an individualized research project, working directly with faculty mentors and subject matter experts.

3
Apply

Scholars join a Local Health Department for a one-year internship in applied public health practice, surveillance, and outreach.

4
Lead

Graduates step into the workforce ready to lead across disciplines, organizations, and sectors to address emerging health challenges.
