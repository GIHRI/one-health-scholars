Mentorship Begins, Feb 2026 Each of the three faculty mentors is matched with a graduate scholar. Scholars are oriented to their project scope, introduced to community partners, and begin structured weekly check-ins with their mentor.
Field Research, Mar 2026 Scholars move into active data collection and community engagement. Projects span Cook County wildlife surveillance, county-level maternal health modeling, and greenspace biodiversity assessment in Markham, IL.
Course Modules Delivered, Apr 2026 Faculty submit four completed One Health training modules to DPI. Each module is reviewed for accuracy, accessibility, and alignment with Illinois public health workforce needs before final production.
Stakeholder Review, Summer 2026 DPI and IDPH host a One Health Stakeholder Course Feedback event. Public health practitioners, local health department staff, and community partners review module content and provide structured feedback before statewide rollout.
Scholars Placed, 2027 Graduate scholars complete the program and enter the Illinois public health workforce as trained One Health coordinators, carrying applied research experience and cross-sector relationships built during the 2026 cohort year.
One Health Scholars

An interdisciplinary program connecting graduate scholars with faculty mentors to advance human, animal, and environmental health research across Illinois communities.
